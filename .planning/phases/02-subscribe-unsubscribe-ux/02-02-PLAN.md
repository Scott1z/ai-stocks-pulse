## Planner decision — where the iOS install-first message lives

`02-UI-SPEC.md` Component Inventory §3 leaves this to the planner. **Decision: the iOS note lives inside the soft-ask banner** as `#pushIosNote`, a sibling of `#pushSoftAskText`, `[hidden]` by default.

Rationale and consequences (plan `02-03` implements this behavior; this plan only provides the element):
- On iOS Safari outside standalone mode, `02-03` will show the banner after the dwell delay with `#pushIosNote` visible and `#pushSoftAskText` + `#pushSoftAskAccept` hidden, and will keep `#pushToggle` hidden entirely.
- A hidden toggle is the honest outcome: OPTIN-03 forbids a "non-functional enable button", and on iOS-in-a-tab there is genuinely nothing to enable until the visitor installs the app.
- The × still dismisses the banner, and the note does not reappear afterward — acceptable, since it is informational and the app's existing install affordances remain.
